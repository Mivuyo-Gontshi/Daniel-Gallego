Organisation's History

Founded in 2020 in Cape Town, Daniel Gallego is a contemporary clothing retail brand that merges African heritage with modern design. The brand began as a small boutique offering handcrafted apparel and has since expanded to multiple physical stores across Johannesburg, Pretoria, Cape Town, Bloemfontein, Durban and eGqiberha (formerly known as Port Elizabeth). Known for its vibrant prints, ethical sourcing, and affordability, Daniel Gallego aims to redefine South African fashion for a global audience.

Mission

“To empower individuals through contemporary, affordable fashion that celebrates African heritage.”

Vision

“To become South Africa’s leading online destination for modern, culturally-inspired apparel.”

Our Values

  • Inclusivity: Celebrating diversity and African heritage in every design.
  • Quality: Commitment to premium materials and craftsmanship.
  • Affordability: Making fashion accessible to all.
  • Ethical Sourcing: Supporting local artisans and sustainable practices.
  • Innovation: Blending tradition with modern trends.

Meet the Team

Our passionate team of designers, tailors, and customer service professionals work together to bring you the best of South African fashion. We are proud to be a locally owned and operated business.
